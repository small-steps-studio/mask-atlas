## Open a sample message

Mask Atlas works with the test messages you already have: **HL7 v2** messages and batches, **FHIR R4 and R5 JSON** (single resources, bundles, NDJSON) and **X12** healthcare interchanges (claims, remittances, eligibility, enrollment, authorisations).

The sample commands open short, invented messages in new editors:

* **Open Sample HL7 Message**: an admission with a patient, a next of kin, a visit, a note and a diagnosis.
* **Open Sample FHIR Bundle**: a patient and an observation with a note.
* **Open Sample X12 Claim**: a professional claim with billing provider, subscriber, payer and a note.

Everything in this guide works on these samples. Nothing in them is a real person or institution.
