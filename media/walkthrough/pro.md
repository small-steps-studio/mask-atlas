## De-identify with realistic pseudonyms (Pro)

**De-identify Message (Pro)** opens a de-identified copy in a new editor; your original is not changed.

* Names, streets, towns, organisations and e-mail addresses are replaced by invented ones. The same original always becomes the same replacement in a run, and two different names, streets, towns, organisations or e-mail addresses never share one.
* Numbers (record, account, member, licence, SSN-like, phone) keep their shape and length. Dates of a patient move by one offset, so order and intervals stay.
* Free text is read for names and numbers seen elsewhere in the same message and for e-mail addresses, phone numbers, SSN-like numbers, IP addresses, dates and labelled numbers.
* The copy is read again and checked. The result says PASS or FAIL and lists, in the report, what the check cannot see.

The first Pro command starts a **free 14-day trial** on this computer. The trial needs no key, no account and no network connection; it is recorded only in the editor's local storage.

Mask Atlas is a helper, not a guarantee. You remain responsible for reviewing the output.
