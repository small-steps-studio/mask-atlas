## What is free

Every feature that is not marked "Pro" is free: **Scan For Identifiers** (the identifier risk report and the marks in the Problems panel), **Preview Masked Version**, **Mask Message In Place** (letters become X, digits become 9), the three sample messages, the walkthrough, the documentation, **Report A Problem**, **Forget Last Run**, **Clear Pseudonym Seed** and the licence commands. The free features need no account, no key and no network connection, and they will not be moved behind the licence key in a later version.

## The trial

The Pro features (**De-identify Message**, **De-identify Selected Files**, **De-identify Folder**, **Verify De-identified Output**, **Export Pseudonym Map**, **Set Pseudonym Seed** and **Create Rules File**) can be tried free of charge in a 14-day trial per computer. The trial starts the first time you run a Pro command, not when you install the extension. It is recorded only in the editor's storage on that computer and is not renewed. **Mask Atlas: Show Licence Status** shows how many days are left.

## Data

Mask Atlas reads and changes your messages and files only on your computer and has no telemetry. The only network request is the licence check with the payment provider after you enter a key; the trial and every message operation use no network at all. Details are in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## A helper, not a guarantee

Masking, de-identification, scanning and verification are aids. They lower the risk that a message points to a person, but they do not remove every identifier: a name written once in free text, an identifier in a field the profile does not know, and details that point to a person only in combination can remain. A verification PASS is not proof. Mask Atlas, its reports and its documentation are not legal advice, and no report is a certificate or an approval of any kind. You remain responsible for reading every result before it is shared and for the rules and laws that apply to your data. The limits are listed in [docs/coverage.md](docs/coverage.md).
